# E-commerce Sales & Profitability Analysis 📊

## Project Overview

This project analyzes 3,500 e-commerce transactions to evaluate sales performance, product and category performance, regional trends, and overall profitability.

The objective was to transform raw transaction data into meaningful business insights and actionable recommendations that can support better decision-making.

## Business Questions

The analysis focused on:

- How is overall sales and profit performing?
- Which categories contribute most to revenue?
- Which products generate the highest sales?
- Which products have the highest demand by quantity?
- Which regions perform best in sales and profitability?
- How consistent are profit margins across products?
- What actions can be taken based on the findings?

## Tools Used

- **Power BI** — Data visualization and dashboard development
- **Power Query** — Data cleaning and transformation
- **DAX** — Calculated measures and analysis

## Analysis Process

**Data Preparation → Data Transformation → Data Modeling → DAX Measures → Visualization → Analysis → Insights → Recommendations**

## Dashboard

The project includes five key analytical views:

### 1. Executive Dashboard
Provides a high-level overview of sales, orders, profit, profit margin, quantity sold, sales trends, regional performance, and category contribution.

### 2. Sales Analysis
Examines sales performance and trends across products, categories, and regions.

### 3. Product Analysis
Identifies high-performing products, product demand, category performance, and product-level profitability.

### 4. Profitability Analysis
Evaluates profit performance, product margins, regional profitability, and the relationship between sales and profit.

### 5. Business Insights & Recommendations
Translates the analysis into actionable business insights and recommendations.

## Key Insights

### 1. Strong Overall Business Performance
The business generated approximately **₦11M in total sales** and **₦1.84M in total profit**, resulting in an overall profit margin of approximately **17%**.

### 2. Electronics Is the Primary Revenue Driver
Electronics contributed approximately **50% of total revenue**, making it the strongest revenue-generating category.

### 3. West Is the Strongest-Performing Region
The West region recorded the highest sales and profit performance among the regions analyzed.

### 4. Camera Is the Top-Selling Product
Camera generated approximately **₦1.18M in sales**, making it the highest-selling individual product by revenue.

### 5. Monitor Has the Highest Sales Volume
Monitor recorded approximately **1,876 units sold**, indicating strong customer demand.

### 6. Profit Margins Are Relatively Consistent
Product-level profit margins ranged from approximately **16% to 18%**, indicating relatively stable profitability across products.

### 7. Sales and Profit Have a Positive Relationship
The analysis indicates that products generating higher sales generally tend to generate higher profit.

## Business Recommendations

- **Optimize pricing and operating costs** to improve the current overall profit margin.
- **Continue investing in high-performing Electronics products** while monitoring the performance of other categories.
- **Maintain adequate inventory for high-demand products**, particularly Monitor and other strong-performing products.
- **Study the factors contributing to the West region's performance** and explore opportunities to replicate successful strategies in weaker regions.
- **Monitor product-level profit margins** and review pricing and cost structures to identify opportunities for improved profitability.
- **Prioritize products that demonstrate both strong sales and profitability** when planning promotional and inventory strategies.
